# Public Content Sources

Use the **Descan Manggar 2026** folder in Google Drive as the source-material home for factual
website content. [Find the folder in Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/search?q=Descan%20Manggar%202026).
The files may also exist in local working copies; those copies are not the canonical source location.

Before writing or changing public-facing factual copy, consult this guide and verify each claim
against the cited source itself. Cite the source title, printed page/table, and the data or fieldwork
period. A publication's year is not necessarily its data year. Treat descriptions of expected
benefits as potential outcomes, not measured results. When sources disagree, do not silently choose
one; follow `docs/SPEC.md` §10 and leave disputed claims out until resolved.

## Verified facts for current public pages

### VYOMATANTRA's work

- The expanded `Monografi Kelurahan Manggar 2026` describes participatory data collection with
  kelurahan staff, RT chairs, and BSU managers. It credits BPS Kota Balikpapan with technical and
  methodological assistance. See PDF viewer p. 3. This PDF exposes no printed page numbers, so
  reference it by PDF viewer page.
- The expanded monografi edition lists 14 active BSUs and contains their profiles: RT 25, 29, 34,
  38, 39, 40, 44, 46, 49, 50, 59, 60, 61, and 62. See PDF viewer pp. 56–58 and the following pages.
  Because the approved edition is unresolved in `docs/SPEC.md` §10, do not publish the count until
  that source conflict is resolved.
- The Monografi's employee list records the Lurah, Secretary, Kasi Trantib L-H, Kasi Pemerintahan
  dan Pelayanan Publik, and Kasi Pemberdayaan Masyarakat with names. See PDF viewer pp. 8–9; the
  extracted pages do not expose consistent printed page numbers. The source lists names/titles but
  not the detailed SOTK duties shown in the previous site copy. Do not publish the NIP or phone
  fields present in the same record.
- The standalone RT/BSU map credits direct field survey by Tim Insos VYOMATANTRA, July 2026. The
  `Manggar Dalam Angka 2026` map caption independently credits a field survey by Tim Inovasi Sosial
  VYOMATANTRA ITK (Gambar 1, printed p. 17, PDF viewer p. 18). Cite the standalone map or its
  publication caption when describing the spatial survey. Neither source establishes complete
  coverage of all RT and BSU locations, so do not describe the map as covering every house.
- These sources support describing the team's work as participatory data collection, field survey,
  processing/compilation for the monografi, and mapping RT/BSU locations. They do **not** establish
  that individual members personally performed each spatial-data task; describe those activities as
  collective team work.
- `Proposal Inovasi Sosial_Desa Cantik Kelurahan Manggar.docx`, Lampiran 2, lists eight student team
  members and proposal roles: Naca Kirana Badarudin (Ketua Tim), Indah Rahma Mihadha (Sekretaris),
  Andi Naufal Nurfadhil (Bendahara), Muhammad Aditya Putra, Muhammad Iqbal Ferhan, and Sulthan
  Farizan Fawwaz (Teknis), plus Andi Mushawwira Muchlis and Feby Anggraini (PDD). It names Riki
  Herliansyah as the dosen pembimbing. Publish only these names and role labels; do not copy NIM,
  private contact details, or individualize the collective fieldwork duties without another source.
- No approved vision/mission statement was found in the inspected profile, Monografi, or proposal
  documents. Keep official statements out of public copy until an approved source is supplied.

### Eco Boba

- The documented July 2026 training repurposed low/no-economic-value **plastic packaging waste**
  into cushion/beanbag filling. Participants shredded plastic, bagged it, and sewed cushions; it is
  not a coconut-shell charcoal briquette program. See `Booklet Pelatihan BSU.pdf`, printed pp. 1–4.
- The booklet reports 58 participants in five groups, with five completed cushions (one per group),
  and a 100% task-completion rate. See printed p. 5.
- The booklet presents reduced waste and possible livelihood/product opportunities as expected or
  potential impact. It says structured post-training monitoring/follow-up had not yet been set up.
  Do not present projected impacts as measured results. See printed pp. 5–6.
- The stated target participants included representatives of 14 active BSUs, Balikpapan Timur
  District, and Kelurahan Manggar. The collaboration named in the booklet includes the district,
  kelurahan, BPS Kota Balikpapan, ITK, Ecco Walibar, Pelita Borneo 38, BSU participants, and the
  implementation team. See printed pp. 3, 7.
- The booklet labels its pages "Hal. 01"–"Hal. 06" and "Page 07", while the PDF viewer starts two
  pages later. Quote the booklet's own page label; the PDF viewer page is two higher.

### Manggar snapshot and spatial reference

- `Manggar Dalam Angka 2026` reports 2025 population of 53,098 (27,310 male; 25,788 female) in
  Tabel 3.1.1 (printed p. 36, PDF viewer p. 37) and its technical narrative (printed p. 34, PDF
  viewer p. 35); 100 Rukun Tetangga in Tabel 2.4.1 (printed p. 30, PDF viewer p. 31) and the
  technical narrative (printed p. 24, PDF viewer p. 25); and area of 3,525.50 hectares in Tabel
  1.1.3 (printed p. 19, PDF viewer p. 20) and the technical narrative (printed p. 16, PDF viewer
  p. 17). This publication prints page numbers in its footer, so quote the printed page and the
  PDF viewer page together.
- The publication metadata states data years 2024–2025 and field collection in 2025/2026. Always
  label these statistics as 2025 where the cited table says 2025; do not call them 2026 observations
  merely because the publication was issued in 2026.
- The 2025 boundary table in `Manggar Dalam Angka 2026` gives north: Karang Joang; south: Selat
  Makassar; east: Manggar Baru; west: Sepinggan and Sepinggan Baru (Tabel 1.1.2, printed p. 18,
  PDF viewer p. 19). Use this table for public boundary copy. The user confirmed **Kelurahan
  Karang Joang** only as the northern boundary on 21 October 2026; the settled rule is recorded
  in `docs/SPEC.md` §8.

## Handling sensitive source fields

Source documents include personal phone numbers and employee identifiers such as NIP. Do not copy
them into public copy, examples, fixtures, or this guide. Publish personal contact details only when
the field is intended for public contact and its publication has been confirmed. Use the official
public office contact channel where possible.

## Updating this context

When new material is added to the Google Drive folder, update this index with its Drive title,
subfolder, reporting period, and page/table references. Move a resolved discrepancy out of
`docs/SPEC.md` §10 only after its preferred source/value is confirmed; keep unresolved questions in
that register alone.

## PPID procedure citation

- [Peraturan Komisi Informasi No. 1 Tahun 2013, official record](https://peraturan.go.id/id/peraturan-ki-no-1-tahun-2013)
  and [official PDF](https://peraturan.go.id/files/bn649-2013.pdf), marked in force by the
  Ministry of Law's regulatory database.
- **Pasal 13, printed page 8 (PDF viewer page 8)** sets the 14-working-day filing period from
  receipt of the PPID superior's written response, or from expiry of the 30-working-day period for
  that response. Cite this article with UU No. 14 Tahun 2008, Pasal 37–38, whenever explaining that
  deadline.
